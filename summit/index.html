One virtual day each year

The Annual Summit

The Outlook, the discussion and the activities happen in one day, ending with a written plan that belongs to you.

The day

Most of the year is reading. One day is deciding.

The Annual Outlook gives the room a shared view of the year. The Summit turns that view into a decision about your own work, business or capital.

You leave with twelve months of direction written in your own words, for your own position.

The plan is the deliverable.

Not a collection of notes and not another recording to revisit later. The day ends with a written plan you can use.

The Live Network exists to keep that plan in motion after the event.

How the day works

One text. Three stages of work.

Read the year
We present the Outlook and the evidence behind its position.
Test the view
The room works through the assumptions, risks and questions that could change it.
Write the plan
You decide what to start, stop or strengthen over the next twelve months.

Three positions

The same year does not create the same decision for everyone.

Career
What to learn, negotiate or pursue from where you are now.
Business
Where demand, policy and capital change the plan you are building against.
Investment
What deserves conviction, what carries new risk and what would change your mind.
