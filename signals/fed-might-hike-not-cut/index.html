The Fed Might Hike on July 29, Not Cut

The Fed might hike on July 29, not cut. If the dollar tightens, every African founder's runway maths just changed.

The consensus says the Fed's next move is a cut. Watch the 29 July meeting for the opposite. Oil has run higher on Middle East tension, the Fed has held for five meetings straight, and a live minority on the committee wants to move up, not down. Put the odds of a hike at roughly one in three. That is not the base case, but it is far too high to ignore if you are pricing dollars.

Why it matters here: when the dollar tightens, every African founder's runway maths changes overnight. Hard-currency costs rise, import bills climb, and the dollar-denominated slice of any raise gets more expensive to service.

Nigeria looks strong on the surface and fragile underneath. Reserves sit at a 17-year high above $52.5bn, inflation has eased toward 15%, and the central bank has held its policy rate at 26.5%. That is stability bought with very high rates, and it holds only while oil revenue and inflows hold.

The funding picture confirms the caution. African startups raised about $1.4bn in the first half, but the deal count nearly halved, from 252 to 146. Capital is concentrating into fewer, larger cheques. Equity is selective, not dead.

Method

Assessed ahead of the 29 July FOMC decision: the implied risk of a rate hike given the July oil move, Nigeria's reserve level, headline inflation and the central bank policy rate, and the first-half total and deal count for African startup funding.

Source

  • Federal Reserve, FOMC statement, 29 July 2026
  • Nairametrics, Nigeria external reserves cross $52bn, surpass CBN 2026 forecast
  • Africa: The Big Deal, first-half 2026 African startup funding data

What would prove this wrong

The rival read is that the hike talk is noise. Oil spikes fade, the Fed holds or cuts, and the dollar softens, in which case a defensive FX-and-debt posture costs you the cheaper equity you could have raised. If Middle East tension de-escalates and crude rolls over, the case weakens quickly.

Next move

Fix your FX exposure before 29 July, not after. Build your raise around revenue and debt rather than betting on an equity market writing fewer cheques. Pick a lane the crowd has left open, and move before the Fed does.
