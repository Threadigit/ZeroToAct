How we produce each Signal

What we do before the intelligence reaches you.

We watch what is changing

We follow decisions, prices and policy changes across money, government and technology. Most of what happens is noise. We look for the few changes capable of altering the conditions around your work, business or investments.

We work out why it matters

We trace how a change moves from an announcement or market price into hiring, demand, funding, regulation, operating costs and returns. That is how we separate an interesting story from a useful Signal.

We write for your decision

Every edition explains what the shift means for career builders, business builders and people investing money. We name the next move, show the sources and state what would prove our reading wrong.

The record

The work stays on the record.

Every Signal is dated, sourced and preserved. Previous calls are graded in the archive, away from the reading flow but never out of sight.
