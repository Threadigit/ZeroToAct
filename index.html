Before you subscribe

Clear answers.

What do I receive?

One free Weekly Signal by email, written to be read in a sitting and used in a decision. Every edition is also kept in the archive.

Who is it for?

Professionals, operators and investors whose work or money is exposed to African markets and global decisions. You do not need to work in finance.

Is the Signal free?

Yes. Weekly Signals are free. Summit and Network details are separate, and you do not need either to read the publication.

How is this different from a news recap?

It does not try to cover the week. It selects one shift, traces how it transmits, states what it changes and names what would prove the reading wrong.
