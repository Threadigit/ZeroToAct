What exactly is ZeroToAct?

A weekly intelligence publication with two things attached. A yearly Summit that turns the context into a plan, and a screened community where cells of six hold each other to what they said they would do.

Are Weekly Signals free?

Yes. One sharp Signal by email each week, free, and every past edition stays archived at zerotoact.com/signals.

What else do you publish?

The Africa Opportunity Map, one country a week. The Policy Tracker, starting with Nigeria. And the Annual Outlook, which grades the year's calls before making new ones.

Who is the community for?

Anyone willing to show up every two weeks and say what they actually did. Cells are matched by stage and by what you are building, so you land with five people at a similar point rather than a random six.

I am a student, or I am early. Is this for me?

Yes. The Signals are the same for everyone, and in the community you are matched with people at your stage, so the cell is useful to you and you are useful to it.

Do I need to join everything at once?

No. Start with the free Weekly Signals. They build the context you will use at the Summit and inside the community.
