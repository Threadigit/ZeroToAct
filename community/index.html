Peer groups of five

The Live Network

Five people at your stage. One standing commitment. A review every two weeks.

The network

Understanding is not the same as moving.

A private intention with no witness quietly expires. The Live Network places you with four people at a similar stage who expect to hear what you did next.

The group meets every two weeks. The commitment stays yours. The record stays visible.

Five people. One commitment. Every two weeks.

Small enough for an absence to be obvious. Large enough to survive someone having a difficult month.

Cells run on WhatsApp. They are peer led, with moderators checking the health of the cadence rather than chairing the meeting.

How it works

The structure is deliberately simple.

Apply
Tell us what you are building, your stage and the decision you are trying to move.
Commit
Set the action and the date in front of four people matched to your position.
Review
Every two weeks, say what shipped, what changed and what happens next.

Useful at three positions

Accountability changes with the work.

Career
Keep a learning plan, search, negotiation or professional move attached to a date.
Business
Review the operating commitment that matters now, from revenue and hiring to a launch or change in direction.
Investment
State the reasoning behind an allocation and the condition that would change your mind.

What it is not

Not another content channel.

No feed to keep up with and no paid mastermind performance. The work is to say what you will do, then return in a fortnight with the record.

The community is live.

Open to anyone ready to commit to the cadence.

See the shift.
Make the move.
